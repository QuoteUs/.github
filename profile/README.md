**QuoteUs** is the quote book for your student house, your friends or your family. Save what gets said, credit the person who said it and share it only with the people who were there.

- **Groups for everyone.** Start a group for your house, your society, your team or your family and invite your people.
- **Always the right author.** Every quote belongs to the person who said it, even if they don't have the app yet.
- **Just for you lot.** No public feed, no tracking, servers in the European Union.

QuoteUs is now in beta on iPhone through TestFlight. Android and a web version are coming later.

[Website](https://quoteus.eu) · [Join the beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/FxUNhBHg) · [Help center](https://help.quoteus.eu) · [support@quoteus.eu](mailto:support@quoteus.eu)

The source code of QuoteUs is not public; the repositories in this organization are private. Questions, or found a security issue? Email [support@quoteus.eu](mailto:support@quoteus.eu).
